Main evidence:
- O(n+L) direct query compared to O(nK+L) cut rerooting.
- 44 controlled timing instances, four tree shapes; complete n<=256, sparse n<=512.
- 500 independent samples x eight simulation settings, both zero and 10 bps costs.
- 18 historical panel/configuration combinations; 10, 30 and 49 industries, 2000-2025.
- 53,472 allocations and 77,245 restricted Clarabel calls; all independently evaluated global gaps <=2e-7.
- 431,574 historical ledger rows replayed independently.
- Primary time-series inference: 1,999 circular-block replicates, 63-day blocks, Holm family of 12 tests.
