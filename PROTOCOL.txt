Protocol fixed before the reported experiments, 3 November 2026.
This is an internal analysis protocol, not an external preregistration.

Question: exact evaluation of quadratic portfolio risk over a one-edge exchange
neighbourhood of a Gaussian correlation tree, and its use in robust allocation.
No confidence-coverage claim is attached to this deterministic stress set.

Theory: tree path-product covariance; exchange factorization; batch path oracle;
rank-four perturbation; convex allocation with a computable tangent lower bound.
All mathematical claims will have complete proofs and independent numerical checks.

Verification: compare every exchange against independently constructed dense
covariances, including signed and zero edge correlations and signed exposures;
compare constraint generation against a full-scenario convex problem.

Performance: compare path oracle with per-candidate linear-time tree messages,
not only dense covariance reconstruction. Trees: star, balanced binary, path,
and random recursive. Report preprocessing and query costs separately.

Simulation: fixed random seeds; stationary Gaussian tree, Student t tree,
one-exchange perturbation, and misspecified factor covariance; n=12 and 30;
training length 126; 100 independent replications per setting. Report paired
population risk and stress risk, turnover and uncertainty, without choosing
settings or hyperparameters for favourable results.

Empirical illustration: Kenneth French daily value-weighted 10 and 30 industry
portfolios. Estimation 252 trading days; monthly rebalancing; 2000-2025 intended
evaluation, limited to complete calendar years actually in the downloaded file.
These overlapping US industry panels are NOT independent datasets, and the
portfolios are research return series, NOT directly tradeable instruments.
Costs are illustrative allocation-layer costs, excluding within-industry trading.
No survivorship-free investable strategy or implementable net alpha is claimed.
All methods use identical information and cost accounting.

Methods: equal weight; HRP; Ledoit-Wolf minimum variance; one tree; local robust
tree. All optimization-based models use the same long-only simplex, gamma=5,
21-day risk horizon, and proportional costs. Main per-side cost 10 basis points;
sensitivity 0 and 25 bps. No fitted return forecasts.
Gaussian Chow-Liu score built from a Ledoit-Wolf correlation estimate.
Main stress neighbourhood: every single feasible edge exchange; include reference.
Likelihood-filter sensitivity may be added and reported without changing main case.

Outputs: exact algorithm verification; running time; solver gap and iterations;
annualized volatility, annualized gross/net mean, wealth drawdown, two-way
traded fraction, gross/net growth and zero-benchmark return/volatility ratio.
Do not label the latter an excess-return Sharpe ratio.
Inference: paired block bootstrap with 63-day blocks, 999 replicates; emphasis
on differences in volatility and net return, no selecting the best cost scenario.

Limitations retained even for favourable results: local (one exchange) ambiguity,
Gaussian-tree covariance misspecification, dependence of estimated scenarios,
floating-point rather than interval-verified bounds, stylized empirical costs,
single-country/overlapping datasets, and no universal outperformance guarantee.
